Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock new theme presets?+
Themes can be unlocked through theme marketplace using virtual coin tokens, which are earned by completing daily and weekly simulation challenge matrix goals.
Are game scores stored locally or remotely?+
All progress database records are locked inside your browser's local sandbox storage wrapper. You can back up your progress by navigating to the Settings panel and exporting it.
How is the BGM generated offline?+
Sound arrays are synthesized dynamically on the client processor using Web Audio API nodes. This ensures offline operability and saves platform resource loading cycles.

Privacy Policy

GameVerse does not collect, sell, or query player personal identity credentials to remote third-party databases. All progression tracking, levels, coins, and achievement unlocks reside in your browser's local sandbox storage registry.

If you choose to sync your leaderboard metrics, a randomized player key is generated to register scores anonymously. No background tracking pixels are deployed.
